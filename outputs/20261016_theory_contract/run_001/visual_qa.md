# Figure inspection

All five new figure families and both rerun compensation figures were opened and visually inspected. Header/legend overlap in the first cancellation chart was corrected. Hard activity changes and the p=0 support boundary are shown without continuous interpolation across jumps; the analytical right limit is marked separately. The probability equality is identified as multiple-optimum, and infeasible SOC cases have status markers rather than invented numerical costs. Time, SOC, currencies and fixed delivered energy were checked against saved records. PNG and vector PDF exports are included.

Original solver records and their hashes were not changed during visual corrections. Final rendering code and figure hashes are in presentation/report.py and figure_manifest.json. CSVs retain their original RFC-4180 CRLF record separators and hash bytes; scoped .gitattributes preserves them through Git checkouts.
